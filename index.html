Tutorials➘

1 - Minecraft is a survival game where you collect resources, craft tools, build, and explore the world. Start by punching a tree to get wood, then craft a crafting table and basic tools. Mine stone, coal, and iron to upgrade your equipment. Build a house before night to stay safe from monsters. As you progress, find diamonds, visit the Nether to collect Blaze Rods, and gather Ender Pearls. Finally, locate the Stronghold, enter the End, and defeat the Ender Dragon to complete the game.

2 - Stardew Valley is a farming simulation game. Start by planting the parsnip seeds you were given, wait for them to grow while watering them regularly, and then sell them. Use the money you earn to buy more seeds!Earn more money and expand your farm. If you get bored, you can head down into the mine with a pickaxe and a sword, or make friends with the villagers by giving them various gifts.

3 - Sky: Children of Light is a fairly simple but relaxing game. First, you'll learn about the game's story through the tutorial, and then you'll be free to meet other players and explore the world. Earn candles and use them to buy accessories from the spirits to upgrade your character.
